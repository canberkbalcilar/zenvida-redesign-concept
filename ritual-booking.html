About this ritual

Foot reflexology in Madrid Centro

Foot reflexology in Madrid focuses on specific pressure points on the feet and calves that correspond to different parts of the body, including the head, lungs, stomach and lower back.

By working these reflex zones with precise pressure, our therapists help the whole body relax. This is the long text area: it is written in WordPress, read by search engines and opened by guests who want the detail.

It can hold headings, lists and links, so each ritual can explain who it is for, what to expect and how it compares with other rituals.

Absolutely. Our rituals restore tired legs and feet after exploring Madrid or a long flight. All our staff speak English.
