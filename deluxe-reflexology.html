An ancient practice

Used for thousands of years in China and India

Your body works as one connected system — and your feet are the map.

Foot reflexology focuses on specific pressure points on the feet and calves that correspond to different parts of the body, including the head, lungs, stomach and lower back. By working these reflex zones with precise podal reflexology techniques, we support the body's natural balance.

The principle is simple: when stress or tension builds up, it shows up throughout the body. Reflexology uses the feet as a map to identify and release that tension.

Reflexology also supports the connection between physical and emotional wellbeing. Working specific pressure points can reduce tension, ease anxiety and lead to a feeling of calm and mental clarity.

For people walking a lot in a city like Madrid, it is especially helpful for easing tired feet, reducing soreness in the legs and lower back, and helping with travel fatigue.

FAQ

Good to know

What is foot reflexology?

It applies targeted pressure to specific reflex zones on the feet that correspond to different organs and systems throughout the body — supporting balance, circulation and whole-body relaxation.

How is it different from a foot massage?

A foot massage relaxes the muscles and tissue of the foot itself. Reflexology works on pressure points that correspond to the rest of the body, aiming for whole-body balance and wellbeing — not just local relaxation.

How does the deposit work?

You pay a €25 deposit per guest by card when you book. The rest — plus any foot bath upgrade — is paid at the studio after your treatment.

Can I book for a group?

Yes. Set the number of guests (2–6) in the booking box and we'll schedule your treatments together.

How often should I come?

We recommend every two weeks or monthly for best results, though this can vary with your needs.

What should I wear?

Comfortable clothing you can roll up to the knee. We provide everything else.
