Before you come

Good to know

Do I need to book?

No — walk in anytime. Booking online guarantees your time and lets you choose your therapist in the Mindbody app.

Which ritual should I choose first?

For tired feet after walking Madrid, start with Deluxe Reflexology. For back and neck tension, try Deep Tissue or Thai. Not sure? Use "Help me choose" above.

What's the difference between reflexology and massage?

Massage works the muscles directly. Reflexology works pressure points on the feet that connect to the whole body, for deep, whole-body relaxation.

Can I come with friends or my partner?

Yes. Couples Massage has two therapists in one shared room, and several rituals can be booked for small groups.
