FAQ

Good to know

What is ZenVida?

A wellness studio for restorative foot reflexology and full-body massage — luxurious, culturally immersive rituals rooted in Spanish heritage.

Do I need to book?

No, walk-ins are welcome. Booking online guarantees your time; a €25 deposit secures it and the rest is paid after your ritual.

What is your cancellation policy?

Please give at least 4 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule. No-shows and late cancellations are charged 50%.

Do you offer couples or group rituals?

Yes. Couples Massage has two therapists in one shared room, and many rituals can be booked for groups of up to six.

Are your rituals suitable for pregnancy?

Our Pregnancy Massage is adapted for every trimester. Tell us you're pregnant when you book so we can guide you.

Is ZenVida suitable for tourists?

Absolutely. Our rituals restore tired legs and feet after exploring Madrid or a long flight. All our staff speak English.
